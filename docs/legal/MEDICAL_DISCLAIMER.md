## 1. Platform Scope & Technology Intermediary Disclaimer

1. **Non-Medical Entity:** Psyco AI is a software technology platform designed to connect patients with independent licensed psychologists and provide administrative documentation workflows.
2. **No Practice of Medicine:** The platform does not practice medicine, clinical psychology, or psychotherapy.
3. **Information Not Advice:** Nothing published on this website constitutes professional medical, psychiatric, or psychological advice.

---

## 2. No Autonomous Medical Diagnosis or Prescribing

- Psyco AI does not independently provide medical or psychological diagnoses.
- The software does not calculate pharmaceutical dosages, recommend psychotropic drugs, or prescribe medications.
- All clinical assessments and treatment decisions are made exclusively by licensed human healthcare practitioners.

## 4. International Crisis Guidance

If you are accessing Psyco AI outside India:
- **United States & Canada:** Dial or text **988** for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or call **911** for emergency services.
- **United Kingdom:** Call **111** for NHS mental health triage or dial **999** for emergency dispatch.
- **Other Jurisdictions:** Proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.
