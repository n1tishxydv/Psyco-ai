## 4. Audio Processing & AI Services (Zero-Binary Invariant)

### 4.1 Strict Zero-Binary-Media Storage Invariant
Psyco AI enforces an architectural zero-binary-media invariant across its own persistent storage:
- Raw audio waveforms and video recordings of consultations are **never permanently stored on Psyco AI disks or database nodes**.
- Speech-to-text conversion uses transient streaming audio buffers that are purged from local server RAM immediately upon transcript text extraction.
- Permanent storage in Firebase Realtime Database consists exclusively of structured text and JSON metadata.

### 4.2 Speech-to-Text Processing & AssemblyAI Disclosures
- **Configured Integration:** Psyco AI integrates exclusively with **AssemblyAI Streaming v3** (`wss://streaming.assemblyai.com/v3/ws`, using short-lived tokens obtained via `https://streaming.assemblyai.com/v3/token`). The platform does **not** use AssemblyAI's asynchronous prerecorded file upload service (`/v2/transcript`).
- **Streaming Retention & Model Training Opt-Out:** Under AssemblyAI vendor documentation for Streaming v3, audio data and transcripts are processed transiently in memory with zero vendor retention when the customer account has opted out of model training (with operational metadata retained solely for connection logging, metrics, and billing).
- **No Presumed Opt-Out or Executed Agreement:** Psyco AI does not assume that model-training opt-out has been activated in the external account settings, nor does it claim an executed enterprise Zero Data Retention (ZDR) addendum or Business Associate Agreement (BAA) without verified documentary proof.
- **Mandatory Pilot Restriction:** **Real clinical audio from actual patients must NOT be transmitted or processed during the staging pilot.** Only synthetic or simulated test audio is authorized until the account model-training opt-out status and contractual data-processing terms are verified.

### 4.3 Google Gemini AI Clinical Copilot Disclosures
- **Deployed Integration:** Psyco AI utilizes the **Google AI Studio developer API** via the `@google/genai` SDK (`gemini-3.8-flash` / `gemini-flash-lite-latest`) using an API key (`GEMINI_API_KEY`). The deployed backend does **not** currently use Google Cloud Vertex AI enterprise infrastructure.
- **Applicable Terms & Model Training:** Under Google AI Studio developer terms:
  - Free-tier / unpaid developer keys permit Google to log and review prompts and responses for product evaluation and safety improvements (including potential human review).
  - Paid / pay-as-you-go projects linked to a commercial Google Cloud billing account are governed by terms providing that customer data is not used for foundation model training.
  - The staging deployment uses developer API keys; therefore, enterprise non-training guarantees cannot be claimed until a commercial billing project is bound.
- **Assistive Drafting Only:** Gemini produces preliminary draft notes (`status: 'draft'`) that are completely invisible to patients and unauthorized parties.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** AI cannot finalize, prescribe, or digitally sign clinical records. Final records require explicit review, modification, and digital signing by the treating psychologist.

### 5.2 Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: Phased Commencement Schedule
The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) commencement is governed by Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology **Notification G.S.R. 843(E), dated 13 November 2025**. The statutory commencement schedule is strictly partitioned as follows:

1. **In Force (Since 13 November 2025):**
   - Section 1(2) (Commencement powers)
   - Section 2 (Definitions)
   - Sections 18–26 (Data Protection Board of India: establishment, composition, selection, terms, meetings, staff, powers, proceedings)
   - Section 35 (Powers of Central Government to call for information)
   - Sections 38–43 (Rulemaking powers, laying of rules, power to remove difficulties, bar of jurisdiction, protection of action taken in good faith)
   - Specified provisions of Section 44 (Amendments to the IT Act and RTI Act).
2. **Scheduled for 13 November 2026 (1-Year Transition):**
   - Section 6(9) (Requirement for Data Fiduciary to issue notice to Data Principals who gave consent prior to commencement)
   - Section 27(1)(d) (Power of the Board to call for information or attendance relating to Section 6(9)).
3. **Scheduled for 13 May 2027 (18-Month Transition):**
   - Section 3 (Application of Act)
   - Section 4 (Grounds for processing personal data)
   - Section 5 (Notice requirement)
   - Section 6(1)–(8) and 6(10) (Consent requirements, withdrawal, consent managers)
   - Sections 7–17 (Legitimate uses, general obligations of data fiduciary, children's data and verifiable parental consent, significant data fiduciaries, rights of data principals: access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, nomination; duties of data principals; transfer of personal data outside India)
   - Section 27 except clause (d) (Powers of the Board regarding inquiry, orders, directions)
   - Sections 28–34 (Inquiry process, appeals to TDSAT, alternative dispute resolution, voluntary undertakings, financial penalties)
   - Sections 36–37 (Power to issue directions, power to block access)
   - Section 44(2) (Repeal of Section 43A of the IT Act).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **STATUTORY TIMELINE CLARIFICATION:** The general Data Fiduciary obligation framework (Sections 4, 5, 6(1)–(8), 7, 8), Data Principal rights (Sections 11–14), and statutory financial penalties (Sections 33–34) do NOT commence in November 2026; they commence on **13 May 2027**. In November 2026, only Section 6(9) and Section 27(1)(d) take effect.

### 5.5 Intermediary Status & Grievance Officer Analysis
- **Intermediary Applicability under Section 2(w):** Whether Psyco AI qualifies as an "intermediary" under Section 2(w) of the IT Act, 2000 involves legal caveats. For functions where Psyco AI facilitates communications or stores records between independent third-party psychologists and their patients (e.g. appointment scheduling, session links, shared notes), Psyco AI may be characterized as an intermediary. However, where Psyco AI provides proprietary clinical tools, intake assessments, and AI copilot drafting, it acts as a SaaS platform and direct Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act. Qualified legal counsel must advise on this dual-characterization.
- **Rule 3(2)(a) Grievance Officer Clarification:** Rule 3(2)(a) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 mandates publishing the **name of the Grievance Officer and his contact details as well as mechanism** for complaint notification, with a 24-hour receipt acknowledgment and 15-day resolution mandate. Rule 3(2)(a) does **not** expressly state a requirement for a physical street address.
- **Appointment Status:** Psyco AI has **NOT** formally appointed a statutory Grievance Officer. The published contact details (`Nitish Yadav`, `n1tishxydv@gmail.com`) represent platform technical contact channels. Formal statutory appointment remains an **unresolved organizational and legal action**.

## 7. Storage Architecture, Encryption & Clinical Lockout

### 7.1 Provider-by-Provider Encryption Realities
Psyco AI verifies cryptographic protections across each connection hop individually rather than asserting universal encryption claims:
1. **Frontend-to-Backend (Browser to Render):** Enforced over HTTPS. Render edge termination negotiates standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3, depending on client browser and cipher suite support).
2. **Backend-to-Firebase Realtime Database:** Enforced over HTTPS / WSS with TLS 1.2+ enforced by Google Cloud infrastructure.
3. **Backend-to-AssemblyAI & Client-to-AssemblyAI:** Ephemeral token generation occurs over HTTPS to `streaming.assemblyai.com/v3`. Client audio streaming occurs over WSS (WebSocket over TLS).
4. **Backend-to-Google Gemini API:** Enforced over HTTPS to `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` via `@google/genai`.
5. **Backend-to-Google Calendar/Meet API:** Enforced over HTTPS to Google APIs.
6. **Firebase Realtime Database Storage at Rest:** Encrypted at rest using Google-managed cloud storage encryption (AES-256 standard).
7. **Provider-Side Container Storage (Render):** Render application containers utilize ephemeral disks encrypted at rest by the underlying cloud hosting infrastructure.

### 7.2 Explicit Disclaimer: No End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)
Psyco AI does **NOT** provide client-side end-to-end encryption (E2EE). The backend application server requires access to plaintext records to evaluate relationship-based access rules (ReBAC), generate audit logs, validate input schemas, and format AI copilot prompts. Claiming E2EE would constitute a fraudulent misrepresentation of cloud healthcare SaaS architecture.

### 7.3 Administrative Clinical Lockout
Administrators are mathematically barred from accessing clinical session notes, therapy transcripts, or intake questionnaires via Express middleware (HTTP 403 `ADMIN_CLINICAL_ACCESS_PROHIBITED`) and Realtime Database rules.

## 8. Clinical Data Retention by Practitioner Type & Applicable Law

Retention of clinical records depends strictly on practitioner classification, patient age, and applicable statutory guidelines:

| Practitioner / Record Category | Applicable Legal / Regulatory Framework | Retention Characterization | Technical RTDB Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Psychiatrists / Registered Medical Practitioners (RMPs)** | National Medical Commission (NMC) Code of Medical Ethics Regulations, 2002 (Regulation 1.3.1) & Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. | **3 years** for inpatient/indoor medical records and digital telemedicine consultations. | Stored indefinitely in RTDB until manual deletion. No automated cron purge currently active. |
| **Clinical Psychologists & Counselors** | National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions (NCAHP) Act, 2021, Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), and State Clinical Establishments Acts. | **3 to 7 years** (State laws vary; NMC 3-year indoor rule does NOT apply universally to outpatient psychotherapy). | **REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW** for state-specific mandates. Stored indefinitely in RTDB. |
| **Pediatric & Minor Patients (< 18 Years)** | Section 6 of the Limitation Act, 1963 (procedural rule tolling limitation period until majority at age 18, allowing tort/negligence claims up to age 21). | **Until age 21 minimum (7 to 10+ years)** as a risk-management / litigation defense consideration, NOT a standalone statutory retention command. | **REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW** to determine formal institutional retention policy. |
| **User Account & Demographics** | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (purpose limitation and erasure upon cessation of purpose). | Retained during active account lifespan; soft-deleted accounts scheduled for 30-day purge. | Deactivated in Auth; flagged in RTDB. |
| **Cybersecurity Audit Logs** | CERT-In Directions under IT Act Section 70B. | Minimum **180 days to 1 year**. | Stored in `/auditLogs` append-only JSON tree. |

> [!NOTE]
> **LIMITATION ACT CLARIFICATION:** Section 6 of the Limitation Act, 1963 is a procedural statute of limitations governing legal disability and lawsuit filing windows; it does **not** constitute a statutory health-code retention command. Retaining pediatric records until age 21 is a medico-legal risk-mitigation practice to preserve evidence against potential claims, not a statutory mandate. All retention periods for non-RMP practitioners and minor records remain classified as **REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW**.

## 9. User Data Privacy Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (subject to statutory commencement) and general privacy principles, users possess:
1. **Right to Access:** Obtain a summary of personal data processed.
2. **Right to Correction:** Request correction or updating of inaccurate personal data.
3. **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data, subject to mandatory medical record retention obligations.
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g., speech-to-text transcription).
5. **Right of Grievance Redressal:** Submit a privacy grievance.
