## 1. Summary of Data Principal Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) and general privacy laws, data principals may exercise:
- **Right to Access (Section 11):** Obtain a summary of personal data being processed.
- **Right to Correction & Completion (Section 12):** Update inaccurate personal information.
- **Right to Erasure (Section 12):** Request deletion of non-mandatory account information.
- **Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13):** Register formal privacy complaints.
- **Right to Nominate (Section 14):** Designate an individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

## 2. Request Submission Workflow

1. Users access the public form at [`/privacy-requests`](file:///c:/Users/Nitish%20kumar/projects/time%20pass/psyco/frontend/src/features/public/PrivacyRequestsPage.jsx).
2. The user selects their platform role (`patient`, `psychologist`, `guardian`, `other`), chooses a request category, and provides details.
3. Submissions are processed by the backend and saved with a unique tracking ID into `/contactSubmissions`.
4. Our platform contact initiates email identity verification to protect health records against unauthorized disclosure.

## 3. Statutory Timelines & Operational Response

Turnaround times will align with rules promulgated under the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 upon formal notification. During this staging evaluation phase, inquiries are acknowledged within standard business hours.
