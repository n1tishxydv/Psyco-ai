## 5. Practice Modalities

Psyco AI accommodates three distinct clinical practice structures:
- **Independent Practice:** Clinician operates independently without a clinic association, conducting online tele-health and independent office visits.
- **Clinic-Affiliated Practice:** Clinician is associated with a registered multi-provider clinic facility under centralized administrative coordination.
- **Hybrid Practice:** Clinician maintains both independent private practice hours and clinic-affiliated hours.
