# PSYCO AI — INDEPENDENT PRACTICE MANAGEMENT GUIDE
**A Comprehensive Practitioner Guide to Operating an Autonomous Psychology Practice**

---

## 1. Introduction: True Professional Autonomy
Psyco AI is engineered to empower independent mental health professionals. You do not need to be employed by a clinic or obtain clinic administrator authorization to run your private practice on Psyco AI.

As an independent practitioner, you hold full operational and clinical control over:
- Your practice branding, biography, and professional credentials.
- Your consultation modes (Online Video, In-Person Office, or Both).
- Your physical consultation offices (optional for online-only practices).
- Your working hours, break times, session durations, and conflict buffers.
- Your incoming appointment requests.
- Your patient relationships and medical charts.
- Your clinical documentation (SOAP notes, treatment plans).

---

## 2. Guided Practice Setup in 6 Simple Steps

Navigate to **Practice Setup** (`/psychologist/practice-setup`) from your dashboard or profile:

### Step 1: Professional Information
Verify your professional designation, board license number, qualifications, specializations, and languages. These details establish your verified clinician status on the Psyco AI patient directory.

### Step 2: Select Practice Arrangement
Select **Independent Private Practice**. This ensures:
- You will **never be asked for a clinic ID**.
- Your practice operates under your own name and direct management.
- All patient appointments, fees, and records are scoped directly to you.

*(Note: If you also practice with an external clinic, select **Hybrid Practice** to enable both private and clinic modes).*

### Step 3: Practice Details & Consultation Modes
- **Practice Name**: Enter your formal clinical practice title (e.g. *Dr. Sarah Jenkins Psychological Services*).
- **Practice Description**: Introduce your clinical approach, modalities (CBT, ACT, Psychodynamic), and focus areas.
- **Consultation Modes**:
  - *Online Video Only (Telehealth)*: Ideal for remote clinicians. Physical office address is **completely optional**.
  - *In-Person Only*: For clinicians consulting from a physical office suite.
  - *Both (In-Person & Online)*: Offers patients flexibility between physical attendance and remote video sessions.
- **Consultation Duration & Buffer**: Set your standard clinical hour (default 50 minutes) and post-session note buffer (default 10 minutes).
- **Physical Offices**: If offering in-person consultations, register your office address. You can add multiple office locations if you consult across different physical clinics.

### Step 4: Clinic Affiliation
Independent practitioners bypass this step. If operating a hybrid practice, you can search registered clinics and request membership.

### Step 5: Google Calendar & Meet Integration
Connect your personal Google Calendar:
- Confirmed online consultations automatically generate a private **Google Meet** video conference room.
- Appointments appear directly on your calendar.
- Personal Google events are never read, stored, or permanently retained by Psyco AI.

### Step 6: Review & Practice Activation
Review your practice configuration checklist and click **Activate Practice**. Your practice is immediately ready to accept patient bookings.

## 3. Daily Practice Workflows

### 3.1 Managing Incoming Appointment Requests
1. Open your **Psychologist Dashboard** (`/psychologist/dashboard`).
2. Incoming booking requests appear in the **Incoming Requests** tray with patient details, date/time, and requested mode.
3. Available actions:
   - **Accept**: Confirms the session, locks the time interval, notifies the patient, and generates a Google Meet link (for online sessions).
   - **Propose Alternative Time**: Suggests a revised date and time slot to the patient if the requested time is inconvenient.
   - **Decline**: Courteously declines the request with a mandatory brief explanation.

### 3.2 Conducting Online & In-Person Sessions
- **Online Sessions**: On the day of the appointment, launch the Google Meet session directly from your dashboard or the **Session Workspace** (`/psychologist/sessions/appointment/:id`).
- **In-Person Sessions**: Patients attend at your designated physical practice office. The appointment card displays the exact registered office location and arrival instructions.

### 3.3 Clinical Documentation & SOAP Notes
1. Open the **Session Workspace** for the consultation.
2. Select your documentation format: **SOAP** (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan), **DAP**, or **BIRP**.
3. Author your clinical notes directly. You can save unfinalized drafts at any time.
4. **AI Clinical Copilot**: You can optionally generate a draft SOAP note from session transcripts or clinical highlights.
   - AI outputs are strictly drafts.
   - You retain final clinical authority to edit, accept, or discard AI suggestions.
5. Click **Finalize Note** to lock the record into the patient's permanent clinical chart.

### 3.4 Patient Management & Charts
Navigate to **Patient Directory** (`/psychologist/patients`) to review all assigned patients:
- Filter by *Active Treatment*, *Awaiting Intake*, or *Upcoming Scheduled*.
- Open any patient's **Medical Chart** to review intake questionnaires, historical session notes, treatment goal trajectories, and standardized screener scores (PHQ-9 / GAD-7).
